**Date:** 2026-08-13 · **Prereg:** research/2026-08-13_trigger_discovery_PREREG.md (aca2427, sha256 f30c11c8…a4bf, committed before cut). EXPLORATORY study; variant set fixed in advance (PB-3/5/7, ST-3/5, TB × depths 15/20).

## VERDICTS

1. **Peak identification WORKS. Monetizing it does not.** Pullback triggers do their stated job: PB-5@20 fires after the true peak 75.2% of the time and PB-7 87.6%, vs 22.7% for naive first-crossing. The peak is findable ex-ante. The economics still fail — see 3.

2. **Stage 1 (2024-25, price-free):** one variant cleared the declared advance bar — **PB-5@20** (tighten-rate 56.0% pooled, 60.7/52.5 by season, n=141). Everything else failed. Notables: **stall triggers are worse than nothing** (ST-5@20 tighten 40.3%, mean net +1.1 — a lead that PARKS at blowout depth keeps expanding; the fine-arena sampling-moment lesson holds in a new domain). The naive reference itself has tighten-rates ~55% — which, given it covered only 39-45% against real prices, calibrates how little a price-free tighten-rate means (the market's cushion is worth ~5+ points of it).

3. **Stage 2 (2026 live spreads): PB-5@20 covers 47.3% [Wilson95 34.7-60.2] n=55 vs 52.38 breakeven — FAILED; no forward prereg earned.** Better than naive T20 (39.2%) but still a losing bet. The mechanism number of the study: **the cushion collapses from −2.7 to −0.2.** At naive first-crossing the book quotes +17.6 against a 20.3 deficit (granting ~2.7 points of expected comeback); at the PB-5 fire it quotes +18.9 against a 19.1 deficit — the comeback allowance is GONE. The market watched the same 5-point pullback you did and re-priced it within the join window. Confirmation is paid for twice: once in the points you skipped waiting for it, once in the price when you arrive.

4. **[this season] rider again:** PB-5 tighten-rate on the 2026 tape is 38.2% vs 56.0% historical — the sticky-blowout regime shows up a second way. 2024 tightens more than 2025 in nearly every Stage-1 cell (TB@20: 71.4 vs 42.1) — compression itself is season-regime-dependent.

5. **Noise cells, named and left alone:** PB-5 Q4-late fires covered 83.3% at n=12 (LOW, late fires against shrunken spreads); untouched.

6. **The single residual worth recording:** the gap≥+.15 conditioner is the best cell in EVERY spread cut across both studies — T15 57.1% (n=21), PB-5 62.5% (n=8), T20 50.0% (n=10) — all LOW, all report-only. It is our core quality-gap signal surfacing in the spread market. If a v3 ever exists, it is "gap-qualified trailer, spread version," it requires a fresh prereg, and it runs on FORWARD tape only. Nothing else survives.
